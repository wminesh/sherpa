Job Title: Senior Product Manager
Location: Bangalore

What you will do
As Product Manager, you will play a key role in our journey to make onboarding, investing easily accessible and affordable to all millennials. You will be conceptualizing and executing ideas that create values for Angel One users. The job will involve understanding customer requirements, creating workflows, and coordinating with design, engineering, marketing & operation teams
- Define, own and drive key success metrics within your product area.
- Track and analyze performance and constantly identify improvements to drive maximum usage, achieve business objectives and create a world-class product experience.
- Design & own product roadmap. Take product ownership from concept and design through to launch and business impact.
- Collaborate with design, data analytics, and engineering teams to drive research, experimentation, and high-impact personalisation strategy.
- Build an in-depth understanding of complex internal systems and identify areas of improvement to enhance user experience and operational efficiency.
- Collaborate with internal business verticals to drive core org KPIs
- Own features and experiments end-to-end, including writing product specs, collaborating to create wireframes, driving cross-functional execution, and sharing insights and results throughout the company.
- Gather, review, and interpret qualitative & quantitative data to uncover new product insights

What you are
- Engineering / Management degree from a Tier 1 institution
- Min 7+ yrs of overall work experience in Product Management in Consumer-oriented tech startups
- Track record in driving core Product KPIs with measurable delta achieved
- Obsession with the mission to improve customer experience
- Proficiency in data analytics
- Experience of building new age B2C/B2B products for large scale
- Strong hold on technical skills
- Data-driven decision- making ability
- Sharp User Research skills - conducting Usability Testing , User interviews & A/B Testing
- Ability to conceptualize, manage and prioritize tasks
- Excellent Stakeholder management skills
- Amazing verbal and written communication skills
