## Full Verbatim Job Description
About The Team:
Razorpay is India's leading full-stack payments and financial services company. The SME Payments Group owns the end-to-end experience for small and medium businesses across 14 product pods: onboarding, activation, payments, dashboard, growth and AI-native products.
Onboarding Infra is the platform layer under every merchant's first mile. It runs sign-up and identity, KYC and verification, and the workflow and state systems that take a business from sign-up to live payments while keeping Razorpay compliant with RBI and CKYC norms. It also powers agentic onboarding: RAY, our AI assistant, verifies and activates merchants through this team's APIs.
You will own a platform whose consumers are other product teams, AI agents and regulators as much as merchants.

What You'll Own:
You will own six platform areas end to end, with a senior engineering team of principal, staff and senior engineers:
- Sign-up and identity: Sign-up, login and user and merchant creation across email, mobile and Google
- KYC and verification: CKYC, video KYC; world class OCR platforms; KYC partnerships
- Onboarding workflows and state: The workflow engine and state machine behind merchant KYC and periodic re-KYC; event-driven state sync to downstream systems
- Data privacy and segregation: PII vaulting, consent, and clean separation of data and services across legal entities
- Agentic onboarding rails: The APIs, tools and guardrails that let an AI agent verify and activate a merchant safely
- Platform reliability: SLOs, incident load, support tickets caused by system issues, and test coverage, owned jointly with engineering

What You'll Do:
- Own the Onboarding Infra roadmap and its outcomes: compliance, verification coverage and accuracy, latency, reliability and cost per verification, all tied back to merchant activation.
- Write PRDs and API specs engineers can build from: data models, state transitions, failure modes, idempotency, retries and SLAs.
- Review tech specs and design docs as a peer, and make the call on build-versus-buy, sync-versus-async and consistency tradeoffs.
- Turn regulation into system design with Compliance, Legal and Risk: RBI KYC directions, CKYC and the DPDP Act. Keep Razorpay audit-ready.
- Pick and manage verification partners (registries, bureaus, video KYC and data providers) on coverage, accuracy, latency, uptime and cost. Design the fallbacks when one fails.
- Build the rails for agentic onboarding: expose verification and workflow capabilities as clean tools an AI Agent can call safely, with evals, guardrails and human review where risk demands it.

What We're Looking For:
Experience and background:
- 4 to 8 years of product management, with at least 2 years owning a platform, API or infrastructure product.
- An engineering foundation: a degree in computer science or engineering is not a mandate, ideally 2+ years as a software engineer, solutions architect or similar before moving into product.
- Fintech, payments, banking, lending or identity experience is a plus.

Technical depth:
- Reads and writes API specs (REST, OpenAPI) and designs contracts other teams depend on.
- Understands distributed-systems basics: event-driven architecture and queues, workflow orchestration, idempotency, retries, eventual consistency and state rollback.
- Holds their own in design reviews: data models, service boundaries, latency budgets, SLOs and failure modes.
- Reads observability data (dashboards, traces, logs) and can triage an incident alongside engineers.
- Fluent in SQL; analyses funnels and system data without waiting on an analyst.
- Knows the basics of PII handling, encryption, access control and data vaulting.

AI and agentic systems:
- Has shipped or prototyped with LLMs and agents: tool calling, agent orchestration, evals and guardrails. Not a future interest; show us what you built.
- Uses AI tools daily in their own work: specs, analysis, prototyping.

Collaboration and communication:
- Earns the trust of principal and staff engineers through technical judgment.
- Works well with Compliance, Legal, Risk and external partners, and turns ambiguous regulation into crisp requirements.
- Writes clear, short docs that ends the debate.

Good to have:
- Built or run KYC, AML, identity verification, onboarding or risk platforms.
- Working knowledge of India's KYC stack: CKYC, video KYC, DigiLocker, and PAN, GSTIN and Udyam verification.
- Has been through RBI regulatory programs or audits, or DPDP Act compliance work.
- Identity and auth experience: OAuth, SSO, OTP login and account security.
- Managed external vendors against SLA-backed contracts.
- Founder or early-stage experience building infrastructure from zero.
