About the job
Job Title: Senior Product Manager – Data Analytics & AI Solutions

Job Overview
The Senior Product Manager – Data Analytics & AI Solutions will lead the development and delivery of data analytics and AI-driven products for healthcare customers. The role involves managing the complete product lifecycle, from understanding business requirements and defining product strategy to solution design, development, implementation, and delivery. The candidate will work closely with business stakeholders, data engineering, data science, and technology teams to develop scalable, reusable, and impactful analytics solutions while ensuring alignment with business objectives and customer expectations.

Key Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end product development and delivery of data analytics and AI-driven solutions using reusable accelerators and technology platforms.
- Define, manage, and execute product roadmaps based on business objectives and customer requirements.
- Gather business requirements and prepare detailed Business Requirement Documents (BRDs) and Product Requirement Documents (PRDs).
- Collaborate with data engineering teams on data pipelines, data modelling, architecture, and solution design.
- Define business KPIs, performance metrics, and reporting frameworks for accurate and actionable dashboards.
- Drive Agile product development activities, including sprint planning, backlog grooming, sprint reviews, and delivery tracking.
- Coordinate with engineering, data science, design, and business teams to ensure successful product delivery.
- Manage stakeholder expectations across clinical, business, and technical teams.
- Identify project dependencies, delivery risks, and technical challenges to ensure timely resolution.
- Ensure analytics and AI solutions are scalable, reliable, and aligned with business outcomes.
- Manage product priorities and ensure timely execution of multiple projects.
- Support product innovation through reusable technology frameworks and advanced analytics solutions.

Candidate Profile
- 4–8 years of experience in Product Management, preferably in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence (BI), or AI-driven solutions.
- MBA or equivalent qualification along with a Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline.
- Strong understanding of product strategy, product roadmap planning, and product lifecycle management.
- Experience in business requirement gathering, BRD/PRD documentation, and product delivery.
- Good understanding of data pipelines, data modelling, data architecture, and analytics platforms.
- Working knowledge of BI and data platforms such as Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Databricks, and Snowflake.
- Experience in defining business KPIs, analytics dashboards, and reporting frameworks.
- Strong understanding of Agile methodologies, sprint planning, and product backlog management.
- Experience working with cross-functional engineering, data science, and business teams.
- Strong stakeholder management, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver projects within defined timelines.
- Willingness to travel internationally for client engagements when required.

Preferred Candidate Profile
- Previous experience in healthcare, healthcare technology, or healthcare analytics.
- Familiarity with healthcare data standards and regulations such as HIPAA, HL7, and FHIR.
- Experience working with AI/ML, data science, and advanced analytics teams.
- Exposure to low-code/no-code platforms and reusable technology frameworks.
- Understanding of clinical data, healthcare operational workflows, and healthcare intelligence solutions.
